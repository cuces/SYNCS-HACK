Lola's Adobo

Recipe

By LolaAdded 12 May 2024

This is the adobo recipe that has been in our family for generations. Lola brought it with her from Ilocos, and it's the dish that fills the house with the same smell no matter which kitchen we're in.

Ingredients

  • 1kg chicken thighs (or pork belly)
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup white vinegar
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole peppercorns
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Steps

1. Marinate the chicken in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves and peppercorns...

  1. 1Marinate the chicken in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves and peppercorns for at least 30 minutes.
  2. 2Heat oil in a pot and brown the chicken pieces on all sides.
  3. 3Pour in the marinade and water, then bring to a boil.
  4. 4Reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 30–40 minutes, until the sauce thickens and the chicken is tender.
